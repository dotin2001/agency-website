# Product Brief

## Product objective

Create a modern creative agency website that uses cinematic storytelling, selected case studies, and purposeful interactive moments to convert qualified visitors into project leads.

## Target audience

- Brand, marketing, and communications leaders.
- Founders and operators evaluating creative partners.
- Prospective clients looking for strategy, production, design, or digital storytelling support.
- Future collaborators, hires, and partners.

## Agency positioning

- Placeholder: agency name.
- Placeholder: positioning statement.
- Placeholder: primary market and industries.
- Planned direction: a creative partner combining strategy, storytelling, design, and selective technology to make brand work feel memorable and commercially useful.

## User goals

- Understand what the agency does.
- Evaluate creative quality and strategic thinking.
- Explore relevant project case studies.
- Learn who is behind the work.
- Confirm credibility through client stories.
- Start a conversation with clear next steps.

## Business goals

- Generate qualified project inquiries.
- Communicate positioning quickly.
- Support credibility with selected work and outcomes.
- Make the agency feel distinctive without sacrificing clarity.
- Create a foundation for future content, localization, SEO, and CMS integration.

## Primary conversions

- Contact form submission.
- Project inquiry CTA click.
- Email or phone contact.
- Case study engagement leading to contact.

## Core differentiators

- Cinematic narrative structure.
- Strong case-study storytelling.
- Selective interactive 3D used with purpose.
- Bilingual Vietnamese and English direction.
- Performance and accessibility treated as product requirements.

## Initial scope

- Product and architecture documentation.
- App Router foundation.
- Planned sitemap and homepage narrative.
- Later implementation of production UI, localization, theme, motion, 3D, CMS, analytics, and deployment behavior.

## Out-of-scope items

- CMS implementation.
- Analytics implementation.
- Production UI build.
- Motion or 3D implementation.
- Localization runtime.
- Theme runtime.
- Client portal or account features.
- Ecommerce or payment workflows.

## Success criteria

- Visitors understand the agency offer and creative standard.
- Key routes support a clear journey from discovery to inquiry.
- Case studies communicate challenge, approach, execution, and impact.
- The experience works across mobile and desktop.
- Motion and 3D enhance comprehension rather than distract.
- Accessibility, performance, and reduced-motion fallbacks are preserved.
