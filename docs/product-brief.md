# Product Brief

## Product objective

Create a modern creative and digital agency website that uses cinematic storytelling, selected case studies, and purposeful interactive moments to convert qualified visitors into project leads.

## Target audience

- Brand, marketing, and communications leaders at growing Vietnamese brands.
- Founders and operators evaluating creative and digital partners.
- Premium consumer, real estate, hospitality, and food and beverage brands.
- Companies that need stronger brand presence and clearer digital performance.
- Future collaborators, hires, and partners.

## Agency positioning

- Placeholder: agency name.
- Agency category: modern creative and digital agency.
- Positioning statement: "We combine strategy, creativity, technology, and performance to build brand experiences that people remember and businesses can grow from."
- Vietnamese positioning statement: "Chúng tôi kết hợp chiến lược, sáng tạo, công nghệ và hiệu quả để xây dựng những trải nghiệm thương hiệu đáng nhớ và tạo nền tảng cho doanh nghiệp tăng trưởng."
- Planned direction: build memorable brand experiences rather than isolated marketing assets, with a modern, creative, reliable, and execution-focused posture.
- Positioning guardrail: avoid presenting the agency as only a design studio or only a performance marketing agency.

## Client problems

- Brand communication lacks differentiation.
- Campaigns look disconnected across channels.
- Digital experiences feel generic.
- Creative output does not connect clearly to business results.
- Brands need both creative thinking and technical execution.

## Core capabilities

- Brand strategy.
- Creative campaigns.
- Digital marketing.
- Social media.
- Content production.
- Website and digital experience development.
- Video, motion, and selective 3D production.

## Value pillars

1. Strategic clarity.
2. Creative distinction.
3. Digital craftsmanship.
4. Measurable impact.

## User goals

- Understand what the agency does.
- Evaluate creative quality and strategic thinking.
- Explore relevant project case studies.
- Learn who is behind the work.
- Confirm credibility through client stories.
- Start a conversation with clear next steps.

## Business goals

- Generate qualified project inquiries.
- Communicate positioning quickly.
- Support credibility with selected work and outcomes.
- Make the agency feel distinctive without sacrificing clarity.
- Connect creative work to business goals without claiming guaranteed results.
- Create a foundation for future content, localization, SEO, and CMS integration.

## Primary conversions

- Start a project.
- Request a proposal.
- Book a consultation.
- View selected projects.

Primary CTA:

- English: Start a Project.
- Vietnamese: Bắt đầu dự án.

Secondary CTA:

- English: Explore Our Work.
- Vietnamese: Khám phá dự án.

## Core differentiators

- Combination of strategy, creativity, technology, and performance.
- Cinematic narrative structure.
- Strong case-study storytelling.
- Selective interactive 3D used with purpose.
- Integrated campaign and digital experience thinking across channels.
- Bilingual Vietnamese and English direction.
- Performance and accessibility treated as product requirements.

## Brand personality

- Modern.
- Creative.
- Premium.
- Confident.
- Human.
- Technically capable.
- Results-aware.

## Brand voice guardrails

- Avoid overly corporate language.
- Avoid exaggerated claims.
- Avoid generic phrases such as "one-stop solution".
- Avoid claiming guaranteed business results.
- Avoid positioning based only on awards or scale.
- Avoid inventing client names, results, or industries not provided.

## Initial scope

- Product and architecture documentation.
- App Router foundation.
- Planned sitemap and homepage narrative.
- Later implementation of production UI, localization, theme, motion, 3D, CMS, analytics, and deployment behavior.

## Out-of-scope items

- CMS implementation.
- Analytics implementation.
- Production UI build.
- Motion or 3D implementation.
- Localization runtime.
- Theme runtime.
- Client portal or account features.
- Ecommerce or payment workflows.

## Success criteria

- Visitors understand the agency offer and creative standard.
- Key routes support a clear journey from discovery to inquiry.
- Case studies communicate challenge, approach, execution, and impact.
- The experience works across mobile and desktop.
- Motion and 3D enhance comprehension rather than distract.
- Accessibility, performance, and reduced-motion fallbacks are preserved.

## Open decisions

- Final agency tagline.
- Exact priority among industries.
- Final service packaging.
- Whether the agency will focus mainly on Vietnam or regional clients.
- Final proof points and project metrics.
- Final agency name presentation.
